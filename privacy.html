Crayon Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Crayon is an AI chat assistant at crayon-web.onrender.com and its Android app. This page explains what happens to your data.

What you type

Your messages are sent to our server and passed to AI model providers (Google Gemini, and free models served through OpenRouter) so they can write a reply. Conversations are kept in the server's memory only for the current session so Crayon can follow the thread. They are not saved to a database and are not used to build a profile of you.

Files and code

Files you attach and any code Crayon runs stay in your browser. Short excerpts of an attached file may be sent to the AI model so it can answer questions about it. Nothing is stored on our server.

Web search and page reading

When Crayon searches or opens a web page for you, the search words or page address are sent from our server to a search engine or that website.

Gmail (optional)

Only if you choose "Connect Gmail", you sign in with Google and grant access to your own mailbox (the gmail.modify permission). Crayon uses it only to do what you ask in chat, such as search, read, label, draft or send, and every change needs your approval first. Your Google refresh token is stored encrypted in our database, linked only to your account, and you can remove it any time with "Disconnect", which revokes the token at Google and deletes it from our database. Email content is sent to the AI model only to answer your request. Crayon's use of Google user data follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not sell Google user data, use it for ads, or let humans read it.

Technical data

Our host logs normal request information such as IP address and time. We use your IP address only for abuse limits (rate limiting). A cookie is set only if you connect Gmail, to keep you signed in.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. It is shared only with the providers named above, and only as needed to give you an answer.

Children

Crayon is not intended for children under 13.

Your choices and contact

You can stop using Crayon at any time, disconnect Gmail, and clear your browser data. To ask about your data, email teamalmostinstinct@gmail.com.

Crayon is an AI and can make mistakes. Check important information.
